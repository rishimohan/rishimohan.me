When di was pregnant with Ivyaan, I really wanted to visit her and be with
at the time of pregnancy. To see the baby and be with him, it was sibling's
first baby afterall

Yesterday, the whole family (Di digitally 😅) was here in New Jersey.
It was an important day, we were ready and with bhai and bhabhi at the hospital.
It was time for a new member to join our home,
the procedure was scheduled at 2pm

The doctor reached at around 2:30ish,
quickly talked to us. Mummy and I were in the room
with bhai and bhabhi. Papa was at reception as they don't allow
more than 2 visitors

Bhai was all sorted with docs, mummy was chilling in the room,
talking to di and giving her updates. Bhabhi was on the bed, a bit weak on the inside,
but fierecely strong on the out

I took photos of them together, for a before, as everything was gearing up

And then came the doctor, she was Indian and quickly
walked us through the procedure and then took bhabhi
to the procedure room, with bhai asked to wait outside

Di was on the call all the time, she didn't want to miss any moment.
Mummy and I were in the patient room, waiting.
Hanuman chalisa was playing in the background.
I was excited. I knew if the baby was going to be a him or her from a day before.
And that made me more excited

Mummy was on call with di all the time,
and di kept asking about everything

A moment later bhai was called inside the procedure room

I remember the other doctor asked me to be
inside the room as I was walking around the door
out of excitement

And then bhai sent a message in the family group

"Baby born 🙂"

![](/images/posts/udayesaa.png)

I couldn't contain my excitement. Was peeking from the
patient room to see if I could see anything

And then some minutes later bhai sent a video of the baby.
I remember seeing it multiple times

It was emotional, very. I'll keep that moment

And then bhai came out of the room, I saw him coming.
I went to him and we hugged, and then mummy

Bhai had become a father, mummy a new dadi, and me a new chachu

And we welcomed a baby girl in our family

This is the first time I had seen a baby being born,
from this close
