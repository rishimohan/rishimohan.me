Making mistakes is normal. You learn, you grow and you make new mistakes

The problem is, when you don't learn and keep making same mistakes again and again

But it's not that simple actually. There are mistakes you sometimes willingly make,
even after knowing you're making one

Because your heart is ready to bear the outcome again. Call it a beautiful mistake,
or an experience, beautiful enough that just to live it you're willing to bear the painful outcome

And it's all your heart, having control on your mind, makes you believe,
that it's fine. Whole lot of philosiphy and you only live once and live in the moment
will validate your mistakes. They have, for me

And yet, whatever in me is it called,
is stopping me from making those

It's tough, because instead of doing the simplest of things,
you fight yourself, to overcome what your heart says.
Surprisingly, it's natural too

And if I were, say 10 years back, I would've made the mistake 
(or maybe I had). The thing that's there in me now, it wasn't there back then

Maybe I haven't learnt enough, or maybe heart has its own way

I don't know if it's right, or wrong, 
but am I being truthful to myself? Yes, 100%.
And I guess that settles the whole question

Just because you can do that thing, should you?
